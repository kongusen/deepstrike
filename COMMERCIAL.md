# Commercial Licensing / 商业授权

DeepStrike is dual-licensed. This page explains the model in plain language;
the binding terms are in [LICENSE](./LICENSE).

DeepStrike 采用双授权模式。本页为通俗说明，具有法律约束力的条款以
[LICENSE](./LICENSE) 中的英文条款为准。

## Who can use DeepStrike for free? / 谁可以免费使用？

- Individuals, for any purpose.
- Organizations (including their affiliates) whose total gross revenue in the
  most recently completed fiscal year was **below USD 1,000,000**.

These users receive the full MIT-style grant: use, copy, modify, merge, publish,
distribute, sublicense, and sell — free of charge.

- 个人：任何用途均免费。
- 上一财年总营收（含关联方）**低于 100 万美元**的组织。

以上用户享有完整的 MIT 式授权：使用、复制、修改、合并、发布、分发、
再授权、销售——全部免费。

## Who needs a commercial license? / 谁需要商业授权？

Organizations (including their affiliates) whose Annual Revenue is **USD
1,000,000 or more** must obtain a commercial license before using DeepStrike —
including internal use, projects developed based on DeepStrike, and products or
services built on it that are distributed or provided to third parties.

A grace period applies: evaluation use for up to **30 days** requires no
license. If your organization's revenue grows past the threshold, the free grant
ends at that point and a commercial license is required from then on.

上一财年总营收（含关联方）**达到或超过 100 万美元**的组织，在使用
DeepStrike 之前须获取商业授权——包括内部使用、基于 DeepStrike 开发的项目、
以及对外分发或提供的产品与服务。

宽限：不超过 **30 天**的评估性使用无需授权。若组织营收增长至超过阈值，
免费授权自该时点终止，此后须持有商业授权。

MIT License and remain available under MIT terms forever. The dual license
applies only to versions distributed together with the new LICENSE.

v0.2.62 及之前的所有版本均以 MIT 协议发布，并永久保持 MIT 条款。
